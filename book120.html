🇯🇵 AIと共に異分野の論文を読み解く (4) 〜ケインズ〜 中学生のための経済学冒険

📢 ジョン・メイナード・ケインズ『雇用・利子および貨幣の一般理論』第12章「長期期待の状態」。
未来が予測できない世界で投資やお金の判断はどう決まる？ 心理・群集・アニマル・スピリッツ—ケインズの洞察を読み解く。

🤖 AIアシスタント「ケイくん」
「この章のすごい所は『人間って合理的じゃない』という出発点。投資は確率計算だけじゃないんだ。」

🔍 長期期待の状態 📖 原著 §I–II へジャンプ

投資の規模は「資本の限界効率」と利子率の関係で決まる。そして資本の限界効率は将来の収益への長期期待に依存する。ケインズは「将来の収益はそもそも不確か。だから現在の状況を将来に投影する慣習に頼らざるを得ない」と述べる。

💡 確信の強さ (State of Confidence) 📖 原著 §II へ

長期期待は「最も確からしい予想」だけでなく、その予想をどれだけ確信しているかに依存する。自信が弱ければ投資は動かない。これが「コンフィデンス」の概念。

📌 ケインズのポイント
投資需要スケジュールを決める重要な要素こそ「確信の状態」。経済学はこれまで軽視してきた。

🏛️ コンベンション (慣習) に基づく評価 📖 原著 §IV へ

株式市場では投資家は暗黙の「コンベンション（慣習）」に従う。すなわち「現状がずっと続く」と仮定し、特別な変化の予想がない限りその前提で動く。これにより短期的には個人にとって流動性が生まれるが、その慣習自体が脆弱で一気に崩れる危険も。

🎭 美人コンテスト理論 📖 原著 §V (美人コンテスト) へ

新聞の美人コンテスト—「最も多くの人が美人と思う人」を当てるゲーム。投資も同じ。自分の判断ではなく「平均的な人が平均的に考えるであろう予想」を予想する。これが第3次、第4次の推測を生み、投機市場を支配する。

🧠 「誰が一番きれいか」ではなく、「みんなが『みんなが美しいと思うだろう』と思う顔」を選ぶ — 株式市場の本質。

🐕 アニマル・スピリッツ (動物精神) 📖 原著 §VII へ

ケインズは合理的計算だけでなく生来の衝動・楽観・やる気のエネルギーをアニマル・スピリッツと呼んだ。新しい事業を始めるのは「確率計算よりワクワクするから」。この自発的楽観が経済を動かす。しかし悲観が広がるとアニマル・スピリッツは衰え、投資は停滞する。

🤖 「行動経済学の先駆け。不確実性の時代に特に重要。」

💧 流動性のパラドックスと投機の台頭 📖 原著 §V–VI へ

証券市場は個人に流動性を与えるが、その結果、長期企業家精神ではなく短期的な投機が支配する。 ケインズは「流動性へのフェティシズムは反社会的」と断じる。 カジノのような株式市場は資本開発を誤った方向に導く危険がある。

📊 結論: 投資は利子率だけでなく、期待・確信・慣習・投機・アニマル・スピリッツで変動する。不況時には政府の積極的関与が必要。

📝 美人コンテスト・まとめ

投資判断は「真の価値」より「他の投資家の予想の予想」を読むゲーム。これがバブルや暴落の原因。アニマル・スピリッツが冷めると経済は停滞する。

🏛️ J.M. Keynes — The General Theory (1936) Ch.12 The State of Long-Term Expectation

I 🇯🇵 長期期待へ戻る

WE have seen in the previous chapter that the scale of investment depends on the relation between the rate of interest and the schedule of the marginal efficiency of capital... whilst the marginal efficiency of capital depends on the relation between the supply price of a capital-asset and its prospective yield. In this chapter we shall consider in more detail some of the factors which determine the prospective yield of an asset.

... We may sum up the state of psychological expectation which covers the latter as being the state of long-term expectation; — as distinguished from the short-term expectation...

II 🇯🇵 確信の強さ

It would be foolish, in forming our expectations, to attach great weight to matters which are very uncertain. ... The state of long-term expectation does not solely depend on the most probable forecast we can make. It also depends on the confidence with which we make this forecast — on how highly we rate the likelihood of our best forecast turning out quite wrong.

The state of confidence, as they term it, is a matter to which practical men always pay the closest and most anxious attention... it is one of the major factors determining the schedule of the marginal efficiency of capital.

III 🇯🇵 慣習・株式市場

The outstanding fact is the extreme precariousness of the basis of knowledge on which our estimates of prospective yield have to be made... In former times, when enterprises were mainly owned by those who undertook them... investment depended on a sufficient supply of individuals of sanguine temperament... With the separation between ownership and management... and with the development of organised investment markets, a new factor of great importance has entered in.

The Stock Exchange revalues many investments every day... Thus certain classes of investment are governed by the average expectation of those who deal on the Stock Exchange as revealed in the price of shares, rather than by the genuine expectations of the professional entrepreneur.

IV — The Convention 🇯🇵 コンベンション

In practice we have tacitly agreed, as a rule, to fall back on what is, in truth, a convention. The essence of this convention lies in assuming that the existing state of affairs will continue indefinitely, except in so far as we have specific reasons to expect a change. ... Nevertheless the above conventional method of calculation will be compatible with a considerable measure of continuity and stability in our affairs, so long as we can rely on the maintenance of the convention.

Thus investment becomes reasonably “safe” for the individual investor over short periods... Investments which are “fixed” for the community are thus made “liquid” for the individual. But it is not surprising that a convention, in an absolute view of things so arbitrary, should have its weak points.

V — Precariousness & the Beauty Contest 🇯🇵 美人コンテストへ

... (2) Day-to-day fluctuations in the profits of existing investments... tend to have an altogether excessive, and even an absurd, influence on the market. ... (4) But there is one feature in particular which deserves our attention. ... Professional investment may be likened to those newspaper competitions in which the competitors have to pick out the six prettiest faces from a hundred photographs, the prize being awarded to the competitor whose choice most nearly corresponds to the average preferences of the competitors as a whole; so that each competitor has to pick, not those faces which he himself finds prettiest, but those which he thinks likeliest to catch the fancy of the other competitors, all of whom are looking at the problem from the same point of view. It is not a case of choosing those which, to the best of one’s judgment, are really the prettiest, nor even those which average opinion genuinely thinks the prettiest. We have reached the third degree where we devote our intelligences to anticipating what average opinion expects the average opinion to be.

... It needs more intelligence to defeat the forces of time and our ignorance of the future than to beat the gun. ... Worldly wisdom teaches that it is better for reputation to fail conventionally than to succeed unconventionally.

(5) ... a collapse in the price of equities... may have been due to the weakening either of speculative confidence or of the state of credit. ... recovery requires the revival of both.

VI — Speculation vs Enterprise 🇯🇵 投機と企業家精神

If I may be allowed to appropriate the term speculation for the activity of forecasting the psychology of the market, and the term enterprise for the activity of forecasting the prospective yield of assets over their whole life... As the organisation of investment markets improves, the risk of the predominance of speculation does, however, increase. ... When the capital development of a country becomes a by-product of the activities of a casino, the job is likely to be ill-done.

These tendencies are a scarcely avoidable outcome of our having successfully organised “liquid” investment markets. It is usually agreed that casinos should, in the public interest, be inaccessible and expensive. And perhaps the same is true of Stock Exchanges.

VII — Animal Spirits 🇯🇵 アニマル・スピリッツ

Even apart from the instability due to speculation, there is the instability due to the characteristic of human nature that a large proportion of our positive activities depend on spontaneous optimism rather than on a mathematical expectation... Most, probably, of our decisions to do something positive... can only be taken as a result of animal spirits — of a spontaneous urge to action rather than inaction... Thus if the animal spirits are dimmed and the spontaneous optimism falters, leaving us to depend on nothing but a mathematical expectation, enterprise will fade and die.

... we must have regard, therefore, to the nerves and hysteria and even the digestions and reactions to the weather of those upon whose spontaneous activity it largely depends.

It is our innate urge to activity which makes the wheels go round, our rational selves choosing between the alternatives as best we are able, calculating where we can, but often falling back for our motive on whim or sentiment or chance.

VIII — Role of the State

I expect to see the State, which is in a position to calculate the marginal efficiency of capital-goods on long views and on the basis of the general social advantage, taking an ever greater responsibility for directly organising investment...
